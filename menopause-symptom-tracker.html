A 90-day menopause symptom log your doctor can actually use

"How have your symptoms been?" is the worst question in menopause care — because the honest answer is a shrug. Memories of hot flashes compress into "a lot?", sleep trouble blurs, and appointment prep happens in the parking lot. This tracker replaces the shrug with 90 days of data.

What's inside

  • Daily log — symptoms, severity, sleep, triggers suspected. Two minutes a day.
  • Trigger finder — after a few weeks, patterns surface: caffeine, alcohol, stress, heat, cycle timing.
  • Doctor visit prep page — a one-page summary you walk in with: frequency, severity, what changed, what you want to discuss.

Why 90 days

Perimenopause symptoms fluctuate on long cycles. A week's diary tells your doctor almost nothing; 90 days shows trends, frequency, and whether what you changed actually helped. It also turns "I feel crazy" into a chart — which helps the person holding it as much as the one reading it.
