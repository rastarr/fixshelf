About The Fix Shelf

The Fix Shelf makes fix-it tools — small, practical digital downloads for the parts of life that fall apart. No apps to install, no subscriptions: every product is a printable PDF or a script you download and use the same day.

What we make

  • Printable planners & workbooks — health tracking (menopause, sleep, burnout, pregnancy), ADHD and focus tools, pet care (puppies, reactive dogs, senior pets, houseplants, gardens), wedding and big-moment checklists, family and home admin, and work/money tools like salary negotiation scripts.
  • Affinity DTF tools — preflight scripts, halftone and texture engines for DTF print shops running Affinity software, sold on Gumroad.

Where to buy

Most products are sold on our Payhip store; the Affinity DTF print-shop tools are on our Gumroad store. Every product page on this site links straight to its listing.

How the tools are made

Each product starts as a real problem — a habit that won't stick, a transition with too many moving parts, a print-shop workflow that wastes film — and becomes a fill-in page or script you can act on immediately. Free starters exist too: try the free ADHD printable sampler (no email required) or browse all products & guides.
