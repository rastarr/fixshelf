A senior dog care log: vet prep, medication tracking and the quality-of-life scorecard

Senior dogs change slowly, which is exactly why the changes get missed: the hesitation at stairs, the skipped breakfast, the restlessness at night. Written down week by week, the trend is unmistakable — and early is when most age-related problems are most treatable.

What's inside

  • Vet prep pages — the running log that turns "he seems slower" into facts: dates, frequencies, changes since last visit. Old dogs often need more vet visits, and every one goes better with data.
  • Medication tracker — morning/night grids for the supplements and prescriptions senior years bring, plus dose-change history.
  • Quality-of-life scorecard — the honest weekly check vets use in end-of-life discussions: appetite, mobility, comfort, joy. Hard pages, written gently, so the hard decisions come from data instead of a bad night.

Who it's for

Anyone whose dog has reached the grey muzzle years and deserves its care run as carefully as its puppyhood was. Printable PDF, instant download.
