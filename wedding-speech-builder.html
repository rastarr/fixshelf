How long should a wedding speech be?

Three to five minutes is the range guests forgive — roughly 400 to 650 words spoken. The builder's hard timer converts that to a per-section word budget, so the speech is short by construction rather than by hope. Longer speeches are remembered, but for the wrong reason.

What should a best man speech include?

A short introduction and how you know the groom, one or two genuinely funny but safe stories, a sincere word about the couple's relationship, and a toast to end on. The best man worksheet walks exactly that arc with prompts per section — and the banned list marks the ex-partner and arrest-story territory before you're tempted.

What should the maid of honor say in her speech?

Typically: your relationship to the bride, one story that shows who she was before this wedding, what you've watched the partnership do to her, and a warm close with the toast. The maid of honor worksheet prompts each part separately, so the speech builds instead of spiralling.
