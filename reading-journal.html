What's the difference between a reading journal and a reading log?

A log records titles and dates; a journal keeps what the books left behind — quotes, reactions, the year's thread. This printable does both: the log for the record, quote pages and TBR space.

What do you put in a reading journal?

The titles, in the log; the lines worth rereading, on the quote pages; the verdict on whether a book landed; and the next pick on the TBR list.

How does a 12-book reading challenge work?

Pick 12 books, aim for about one a month, and color a square each time you finish. The grid makes it visible; the log keeps the receipts. Enough to notice a reading year, without homework.
