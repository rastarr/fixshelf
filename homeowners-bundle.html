Is a home maintenance calendar worth it?

Most expensive house problems are deferred cheap ones: a gutter check skipped in autumn becomes a fascia repair in spring. A maintenance calendar turns that pattern into a monthly circuit — the tasks are small, scheduled and checked off, and the repair log starts showing you which problems your actual house keeps having.

Where should you keep an emergency binder?

Somewhere known and reachable without power or passwords: a kitchen shelf or hall cupboard works better than a home safe if the people who need it include a babysitter or a neighbour. The binder includes a page for writing down where it lives — the step most families skip.
