How do I stop overthinking at night?

Keep a pad on the nightstand and offload the loop in one line each — the thought, not the argument. Night overthinking feeds on having nowhere to put things; giving each worry a line on paper ends the rehearsal. The workbook's nightly offload page gives that pad a structure, so morning-you can sort what was real.

What is the difference between overthinking and problem-solving?

Problem-solving ends in a decision or an action; overthinking repeats without producing one. The test is simple: if you've circled the same thought three times with nothing new written down, you're looping, not solving. The workbook's decision filter is built to force that exit — two questions, then a choice you can act on.
