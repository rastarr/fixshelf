How do I track multiple medications on one chart?

One list keeps every person and every medication on a single page, and the daily check-off grid gives each item its own row to tick. When more than one person takes more than one thing, the chart — not memory — holds the answer.

What should a medication log include?

What the medication is, who takes it, a daily check-off for the week, and a refill tracker so you know when a supply runs low. Bring the filled log to appointments instead of reconstructing it from memory.

How do I chart medication taken 4 times a day?

The check-off grid runs a column per daily dose time, so an item taken four times a day ticks four columns and a once-a-day item ticks one. Informational only: a household log, not medical advice.
