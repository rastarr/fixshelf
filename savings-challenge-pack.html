How does the 100 envelope challenge work?

You number envelopes 1 through 100 and tuck the matching dollar amount into each one — envelope one is the smallest, envelope one hundred the biggest. The grid gives every envelope a row to tick as it fills, so the challenge lives on paper instead of in your head.

How much money is the 100 envelope challenge?

The method, not a promise: every envelope from 1 through 100 carries its own amount, the chart lays each one out as a row to tick, and how far you take it is your call — partial challenges work too.

What is the 52 week savings challenge?

A year-long ladder where each week adds a set amount, starting small. The pack prints the ladder with color-in rungs — bank a week, color a rung, and progress stays visible even in the tight weeks.
