What is the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique?

Name five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell, and one you can taste. It works because panic recruits all of attention; giving each sense a job takes that attention back. Our toolkit includes it as a card you can keep in a wallet, so you don't have to remember the steps mid-panic.

Do grounding techniques actually work for anxiety?

They interrupt the spiral in the moment — they pull attention back to the present through the senses. They are a skill, not a cure: they work better with practice, and they don't replace care from a professional if anxiety is frequent or severe. Our cards exist for the moments when remembering the steps is the hard part.
